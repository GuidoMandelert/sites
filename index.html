01 / Background

My Story

I study mechatronics engineering, a field that brings together mechanical systems, electronics, and computing to address practical questions of design and operation. I am drawn to the discipline because it connects technical detail with the wider process of turning an idea into something that works.

My decision to come to China grew from a wish to understand its culture through firsthand experience. Rather than relying only on views formed at a distance, I wanted an opportunity to listen, observe, and learn within a different cultural environment.

This choice also reflects my interest in technology, innovation, and global development. Living and studying in a new context is an ongoing opportunity to broaden my perspective on how people and societies approach progress.

02 / Practice & tools

Engineering & Technical Interests

Mechatronics brings practical problem-solving into conversation with design, manufacturing, electronics, and automation. My interests span the connections between these areas.

01

Electronics & Embedded Systems

I have practical experience with electronics and previously held responsibility for the electronics side of Fox Baja, my home university’s Baja program. My embedded development experience includes PlatformIO and C/C++ programming.

02

CAD & 3D Design

Siemens NX, Autodesk Fusion 360, and Rhino 3D support my 3D design and modelling work, helping me explore form, fit, and design intent.

03

Manufacturing & Materials

I consider material properties and application requirements alongside design decisions. My interests include subtractive manufacturing with CNC machines, lathes, and milling machines.

04

Additive Manufacturing

3D printing provides a distinct, additive approach to making and testing physical forms alongside subtractive methods.

05

Industrial Automation

My automation-related tools and knowledge include TIA Portal, Factory I/O, ladder-logic programming, and the fundamentals of machinery automation.

03 / A reason to look closer

Why China?

Cultural understanding

I came to deepen my understanding of Chinese culture through direct experience, while remaining open to the complexity and variety of perspectives that any society contains.

Technology & innovation

I am interested in the technological development of a country I believe will have an increasingly important role in global innovation, and in learning from that development at closer range.

Engineering & global perspective

This experience helps connect my engineering education with a broader interest in how different societies approach technology, production, and development.

Looking forward

Engineering is also a way of learning to see more broadly.

My time in China is an opportunity to develop a wider perspective on technology, learning, and the world in which engineering takes place.
